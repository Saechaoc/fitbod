# Missing screens

These are the screens and states that the [workflows](workflows/) need and the app does not have.
Each row names the workflow that needs it, what it must show, where the lifter would open it, how much it matters and the evidence.
Importance is `must` when a job cannot be finished well without the screen, `should` when the job is much harder without it, and `nice` otherwise.
Each `must` row has a BRU mock-up in [mockups/](mockups/).
The Milestone 1 removals and the four open product decisions in `docs/ROADMAP.md` have rows too, so the next design can settle each one in one place.
