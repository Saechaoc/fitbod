BRU replaces them with uppercase letterspaced labelSmall text inside ink-outlined chips." Per UI-SPEC line 350-351: "SetCompletionGlyph (hollow bruInk 1.5pt outlined 22pt square / filled bruSignal 22pt square)" replaces the SF Symbol checkmark. The SetCompletionGlyph primitive already exists from Plan 02.1-03.
