## Decisions Made

- **4-cell toggle chrome vs 2-cell functional contract:** Rendered all 4 cells (e1RM / TOP SET / VOLUME / AVG RPE) matching the mockup exactly. e1RM and VOLUME are no-op buttons with a TODO comment — the visual contract is fully met, data wiring is deferred.
