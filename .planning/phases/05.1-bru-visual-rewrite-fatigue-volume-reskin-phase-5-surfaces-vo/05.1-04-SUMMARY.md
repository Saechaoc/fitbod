## Decisions Made

- Switched `MuscleRegionPaths` from `[String: Path]` dictionary to `[GridRegion]` array — enables duplicate slugs for symmetrical pairs (CHEST L + CHEST R both slug `"chest"`) and per-region display labels without a separate lookup table.
- NECK decorative slug `"neck-decorative"` — keeps the region on-screen (for anatomical completeness) while opting it out of zone data and tap routing via `hasSuffix("-decorative")` guard.
- Coverage test updated to 14 grid-visible slugs (not all 17) — `abductors` and `adductors` are absent from `heatmap.jsx` verbatim; adding phantom grid regions to satisfy the 17-slug invariant would violate the "coordinates verbatim" contract.
- Fixed 140×168pt frame (not GeometryReader-scaled) — `FatigueSurfaceView` (plan 05.1-05) owns layout of both side instances side-by-side; the silhouette no longer self-sizes.
