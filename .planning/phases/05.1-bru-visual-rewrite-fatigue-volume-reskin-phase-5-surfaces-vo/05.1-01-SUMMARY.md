## Decisions Made

- `MuscleVolumeBar` drops the Phase 5 two-tone D-07 direct/indirect fill in favor of single-color fill matching `bru-screens/volume.jsx` — BRU visual contract supersedes the iOS-native two-tone encoding. The zone color is still the sole encoding; accessibility label and chip preserve full context.
- Advisory ring switches from `RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: 4)` to `Rectangle().stroke()` per BRU sharp-corner rule, replacing the `Color("VolumeNearMRVAmber")` reference with `Color.bruAmber`.
- Internal computed properties (`chipLabel`, `chipShouldFill`, `isNotTrained`, `accessibilityDescription`) exposed rather than kept private — enables pure-value unit testing with no ViewInspector or XCUIApplication dependency.
